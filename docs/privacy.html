Privacy Policy

mPractice for Canadian Citizenship (iOS app) by mStudio Solutions · Last updated 9 October 2026

The short version

mPractice does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads and no cookies. The app does not collect any data at all.

What we collect

No personal information. All questions are built into the app. Your answers and scores are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your practice history

Your recent scores, your settings and the questions you have already done are saved only on your device, so you can carry on next time. They are never sent to us. You can remove them with the Clear button under Recent Sessions, or by deleting the app.

Cookies, analytics and ads

The app has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. This website (the support and privacy pages) has none of them either.

Purchases

Unlimited practice is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled by Apple. We never see your name, your Apple ID or your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical data (such as your IP address) for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The iOS app is downloaded from the App Store; Apple's own privacy policy applies to the App Store.

Children

mPractice does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Links to other sites

The app and this website link to the Government of Canada website (canada.ca). Their own privacy policy applies when you visit it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
