Support

mPractice for Canadian Citizenship is an iPhone app to practise for the Canadian citizenship test.

Contact us

Email mstudiosolutions@gmail.com. Please tell us your iPhone model and what happened. We usually reply in a few days.

How does the free version work?

You get 1 practice session a day, in any mode. The session is counted when you answer the first question. A new free session is ready the next day.

What do I get when I unlock?

Unlimited practice, every day, in every mode. You pay once. There is no subscription.

I paid, but the app is still locked

Open the app, press the Settings button (the gear at the top right), then press Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you paid with. This also works on a new iPhone or after you delete the app.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Press the Settings button (the gear at the top right) and choose a language. The menus change. The questions and answers stay in English, because the real test is in English or French.

Is this the official test?

No. mPractice is an unofficial practice tool. It is not made by, linked to or approved by the Government of Canada or IRCC. The questions are based on the study guide Discover Canada and are not the real test questions. Always check canada.ca before your test.

The app does not collect any data. See the Privacy Policy.
