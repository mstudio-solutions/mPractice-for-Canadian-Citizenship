## Description (4000)

Get ready for the Canadian citizenship test with mPractice.

mPractice has 501 practice questions based on the official study guide Discover Canada: The Rights and Responsibilities of Citizenship. Every question has a short explanation, so you learn the answer, not just guess it.

FULL MOCK TEST
- 20 questions, like the real test
- You need 15 correct (75%) to pass
- Answers and explanations at the end, like the real test

PRACTISE YOUR WAY
- 10 study guide topics: rights and responsibilities, who we are, history, modern Canada, government, elections, justice, symbols, economy and regions
- Random mode with no repeats until you have seen every question
- Key questions that people often get wrong
- Multiple choice and True/False questions
- Timer per question (30s to 135s real exam pace) or no timer
- Shuffle answers, with large buttons that never move

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
- Your recent scores and progress are saved on your iPhone
- Carry on where you stopped

UP TO DATE FOR 2026
- Where older copies of the guide are out of date (for example King Charles III, the 2021 oath, 343 electoral districts), the explanation tells you both

PRIVATE AND SIMPLE
- Works offline
- No sign-up, no ads, no tracking
- No data collected

mPractice is an unofficial practice tool. It is not made by, linked to or approved by the Government of Canada or Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). The questions are not the real test questions. Always check canada.ca for the latest rules.

Made by mStudio Solutions. Our apps are made for everyday people: clear layouts, large text and no clutter, so they're easy to use at any age.
