What's new in 2026 (last checked October 2026)
  • The test: 20 questions, 45 minutes, pass mark 75% (15 correct). Questions are multiple choice or True/False, in English or French.
  • Most people take the test online, with a webcam, within 30 days of the invitation. You have 3 chances to pass.
  • Applicants aged 18 to 54 must take the test.
  • Discover Canada is still the only official study guide. No new guide has been published yet.
  • Some facts in older copies of the guide are now out of date (for example, the Sovereign is now King Charles III, the oath changed in 2021, there are now 343 electoral districts, and NAFTA was replaced by CUSMA). Where this matters, the explanations in this app tell you both.
  • Always check the official IRCC website (canada.ca) for the latest rules before your test.

Introduction

mPractice for Canadian Citizenship helps you get ready for the Canadian citizenship test. Practise as many times as you like with questions based on the official study guide Discover Canada: The Rights and Responsibilities of Citizenship. Large buttons and clear text make it easy to use for everyone.

Features

  • 0 practice questions across all 10 study guide topics
  • Full Mock test – 20 questions, 75% (15 correct) to pass, like the real test
  • Multiple choice and True/False questions
  • Practice by chapter, Key questions or Random
  • Timer per question, or untimed
  • Fixed answer layout, with optional shuffle
  • Review your mistakes with explanations
  • No sign-up, no ads, no cookies
